To my Livster

Liv, one of the things I love most about you is how much heart you have. You are one of the smartest people I know, and I admire how you keep fighting for the things and people that matter to you. You never back down, and you have a way of making me feel seen, heard, and understood. I hope I make you feel that same kind of love every day.

You came into my life and stood beside me through some of my darkest days. I don't think I could ever fully explain how much that has meant to me. You have reminded me what it feels like to be loved and supported. Being with you has taught me that love isn't always exactly 50/50. Sometimes one of us has to carry a little more, and what matters is that we keep showing up for each other. I want to keep doing that with you.

I can't wait to make more memories with you, starting with our cruise and all the places we still have left to see. More than anything, I look forward to the life ahead of us — building a home, making plans, and one day getting to call you my wife. No matter how busy my day gets, getting to see you is still my favorite part of it.

I love you more than I probably tell you, and more than I could ever fit into these pages. I'm so glad it's you, Liv.

Forever your Brenster ♡
